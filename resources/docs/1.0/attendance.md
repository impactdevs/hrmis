<a name="overview"></a>
## Overview

The **Attendance** module shows daily clock-in/clock-out records collected automatically from the badge/access-control system. It is only accessible to **HR** and the **Executive Secretary**.

---

<a name="viewing-attendance-records"></a>
## Viewing Attendance Records

1. In the sidebar, click **Attendance**.
2. Each row summarizes one staff member's attendance for one day: their earliest scan (**Clock In**), latest scan (**Clock Out**), and the hours between them.
3. By default, the current month is shown. Use the date range fields to view a different period.

---

<a name="filtering"></a>
## Filtering

- **Date Range**: Choose a **From** and **To** date to narrow the records shown.
- **Department** (HR only): Filter records to a single department.
- **Staff Member**: Filter records down to a single employee.

---

<a name="exporting-attendance"></a>
## Exporting Attendance

1. Set the date range and any filters you want applied to the export.
2. Click the **Export** button.
3. Choose **CSV** or **PDF**. The export includes staff ID, name, department, date, clock in, clock out, and hours worked for every matching record.
