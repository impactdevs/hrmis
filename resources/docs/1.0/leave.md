<a name="overview"></a>
## Overview

The **Leave Management** section allows employees to submit and track leave requests, and allows HR, Heads of Division, and the Executive Secretary to review, approve, reject, and — for HR only — schedule and edit leave on behalf of staff.

**Admin View** (HR / Head of Division / Executive Secretary): Can view leave applications submitted by other employees, approve or reject them, and see the approval progress at each stage of the hierarchy.
**Employee View**: Can view their own leave history and submit new leave applications.

---

<a name="applying-for-leave"></a>
## Applying for Leave

1. At the top right of the leave table, click the **Apply** button.
2. Fill out the leave type, dates, handover note (and optional handover file), and who will cover your work.
3. Submit the form. The request is routed for approval according to your reporting hierarchy.

This button is available for applying to leave types other than annual leave, which is instead scheduled from the [Leave Roster](/{{route}}/{{version}}/leave-roster).

---

<a name="scheduling-leave-on-behalf-of-staff"></a>
## Scheduling Leave on Behalf of Staff (HR)

HR users can submit a leave request on behalf of any staff member, not just themselves:

1. Click the **Apply** button as usual.
2. In the **Staff Member** field (only shown to HR), select the employee the leave is being recorded for.
3. Complete the rest of the form and submit.

The leave request is created and routed for approval as if the selected staff member had applied for it themselves.

---

<a name="editing-a-leave-request"></a>
## Editing a Leave Request

A leave request can be edited only while it has not yet been approved.

- **Employees** can edit their own pending leave requests.
- **HR** can additionally edit any staff member's pending leave request, including ones the staff member submitted themselves.

To edit, open the leave request and click the **Edit** button. If the leave has already been approved, editing is disabled and a message explains why.

---

<a name="approving-or-rejecting-a-leave-request"></a>
## Approving or Rejecting a Leave Request

HR, Head of Division, and Executive Secretary users can approve or reject a leave request directly from its detail page:

1. Open the leave request (click the **eye icon** from the leave table, or select it from the leave roster).
2. If you are an eligible approver for the current stage, **Approve** and **Reject** buttons appear near the bottom of the page.
3. Click **Approve** to move the request to the next stage of the approval hierarchy, or **Reject** to open a dialog where you must provide a rejection reason.

The same Approve/Reject actions are also available from the leave requests table via the action icons in the table row.

---

<a name="additional-features"></a>
## Additional Features

- **Copy Table Contents**: Copy the contents of the leave table to the clipboard.
- **Export Options**: Export the leave table to **CSV** or print it for record-keeping.
- **Filters**: HR and the Executive Secretary can filter the leave table by department, year, month, and approval status.
